## How it works

A Wokwi project (https://wokwi.com/projects/455303592417686529) built as a micro tile. The tools fetch its Verilog
with the micro tile port list (`clk`, `rst_n`, `ui_in`, `uo_out`).

## How to test

Follow the Wokwi diagram: drive the inputs and watch the outputs.
